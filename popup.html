Understanding your assessment

Use these labels to understand the findings and decide what to check next. The fit score is an estimate, not your chance of getting hired.

⛔ 1 blocker
One potentially disqualifying mismatch with a mandatory condition was identified. Open its evidence and verify the details before deciding.
Warning
An important condition in the posting, such as “visa sponsorship is unavailable.” It does not automatically mean you are ineligible.
Info
A neutral note from the posting, rather than a problem with your fit.
6 of 8 matched
Supporting evidence was found for six of eight extracted requirements. The remaining two may be gaps or simply absent from your CV. This is not a hiring probability.
Direct evidence / CV evidence found
A supporting passage was found in your CV. Open the evidence to check whether it supports the assessment.
Experience you added / self-reported experience
The finding uses experience you added through “I have this experience,” rather than a passage in your saved CV. Your clarification is shown separately.
Ambiguous in Post
The employer’s wording is unclear—for example, “strong technical background” without specific skills. Ask the recruiter to clarify.
Blocker
A potentially disqualifying mismatch with a mandatory requirement. Missing information alone should not count as a proven blocker.
Risk
An important concern that may need clarification or could be addressed.
Minor
A smaller mismatch, usually involving a desirable rather than essential qualification.
Not evidenced in CV
Your CV does not establish that you meet the requirement. This does not mean you lack the skill. Choose “I have this experience” to add relevant details and reassess.
Confirmed gap
The assessment found explicit evidence that a requirement is not met, or you selected “I need to learn this.” Review the evidence to check the finding.
View supporting evidence
Expands the relevant job-posting quote, CV quote, and any clarification you supplied. Quotes are checked against the supplied text and kept in their original language. If a supporting passage cannot be verified, the panel says so.
Hard skills and soft skills
Hard skills cover tools, technologies and methods. Soft skills cover behaviours such as communication, collaboration and problem-solving. A concrete example is stronger evidence than a generic self-description.
Partial evidence / related experience
Some relevant experience was found, but it does not fully establish the exact requirement. Experience with a related tool is not automatically a direct match.
Placement suitability
Checks your saved dates, duration, hours, return to study and computing-project needs separately from the skills score. Only an evidenced, non-negotiable conflict is shown as a potential blocker. Missing details need clarification.
Evidence needs review
Some findings could not be supported, or requirements were left unassessed. Review the evidence and clarify what is missing before relying on the score.

Your clarifications are saved for this posting and CV version. You can remove them and reassess; they do not edit your original CV.
